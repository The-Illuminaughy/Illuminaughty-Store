Equal Employment Opportunity

Effective Date: 1 January 2024Last Updated: 7 October 2026

Illuminaughty, LLC is committed to equal employment opportunity and a professional workplace free from unlawful discrimination, harassment, and retaliation.

1. Equal Opportunity

Employment decisions are made without unlawful discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, military or veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

2. Harassment-Free Workplace

Illuminaughty, LLC prohibits unlawful harassment, including sexual harassment, and expects employees, applicants, contractors, vendors, and other workplace participants to conduct themselves professionally.

3. Retaliation Prohibited

Retaliation against a person for raising a good-faith concern, requesting an accommodation, participating in an investigation, or exercising rights protected by law is prohibited.

4. Reasonable Accommodations

Illuminaughty, LLC will consider reasonable accommodations for qualified individuals with disabilities and for sincerely held religious practices when required by law, subject to applicable legal standards.

5. Military and Veteran Protections

Illuminaughty, LLC recognizes employment protections applicable to service members and veterans and will comply with applicable federal and state requirements.

6. Reporting Concerns

Workplace concerns involving discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or accommodations should be reported promptly so they can be reviewed and addressed appropriately. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible consistent with a fair review.

7. No Contract of Employment

This policy is a statement of workplace principles and does not create a contract of employment or alter any lawful employment relationship.
